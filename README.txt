Learning Arcade — interactive algebra classroom

Open index.html to choose a class. Only Algebra currently has content.
Algebra has exactly 12 topics. Two are available; ten are coming soon:
- One-variable equations: Balance Lab (simulation), Mystery Island and Space Race (games).
- Linear equations in two variables: Intersection Lab (simulation), Rail Rescue (game).
Each topic opens a Simulation / Game chooser. Every activity includes English and Somali.

Simulations let learners change values and observe relationships without scoring.
Games use an endless generated sequence of problems, with whole numbers, fractions,
addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and varied variable names.
Difficulty builds from simple starter problems. Questions may occasionally recur by chance.

Mystery Island is an endless equation platformer. Three equation platforms fall
at once. Solve the highlighted nearest platform above Nori to jump onto it.
Wrong answers do not trigger a jump. Platforms speed up as you climb; reaching
the spikes ends the run. Touch the four answers or use keys 1–4. Pausing, changing
language, or hiding the tab freezes the action.

Space Race is local two-player: pilot one uses A/S/D/F; pilot two uses J/K/L/;.
Touch choices also work. Solve the meteor equation to launch a missile and travel farther.

Rail Rescue gives two railway equations. Enter their shared ordered pair to switch
both trains onto safe bypass tracks before time expires. Fractions and decimals work.
Practice mode removes the deadline. Train models and city names are real; junctions,
routes shown on the coordinate grid, and timings are fictional educational puzzles.
Train carriages and cover are drawn as crisp vector illustrations, not photographs or official imagery.

Sunny Sips has been removed.
No installation, account, tracking, or server-side game state is required by these activities.
